Past, Present, and Future

Each scenario is a snapshot of an object moving at constant velocity, but every snapshot is part of a longer story. Past: how did it get to its current motion? Present: what forces keep its velocity constant? Future: what would it take to change its velocity?

Press ▶ Play or drag the slider to watch the whole story. Look for the pattern: when the forces are balanced, ΣF = 0 and the velocity stays the same. When they are unbalanced, ΣF points the same way as Δv.
